## v0.1.0: Private Testing

Released on November 10, 2026

Welcome to the first live version of the platform 🎉 This release introduces the core tools to help landlords and property managers track tenants, rent, and payments in one place.

This version is currently in **private testing**, and we’re actively improving it based on real usage and feedback.

---

## 🏠 Property & Tenant Management

- Create and manage properties
- Add, edit, and track tenants
- Assign tenants to properties
- Upload and manage tenant documents
- Prevent accidental deletion of properties with active tenants

---

## 💳 Payments & Rent Tracking

- Automatically generate rent records
- Track expected vs received payments
- Flexible payment durations (1–12 months)
- Clear rent ledger for each tenant

---

## 👥 Team & Access Control

- Invite team members to your company
- Assign roles and control access levels
- Viewer roles restricted to their own profile

---

## 🔐 Security & Authentication

- Secure login and signup
- Password reset functionality
- Role-based access control
- Automatic logout on unauthorized access

---

## 📊 System & Reporting

- Activity logging for key actions
- Basic reports available
- Improved visibility across properties and tenants

---

## ⚙️ Improvements & Experience

- Responsive design across devices
- Improved error handling and notifications
- Cleaner and more consistent inputs
- Faster navigation between pages

---

## 🧪 Private Testing Notice

This version is part of our **early testing phase**. You may encounter bugs or incomplete features as we continue to improve the system.

## **Payments for subscriptions are not live yet, so no payments will be processed for purchasing a subscription as of yet!!!**

Your feedback is extremely valuable and will directly shape future updates.

---

## 🔜 Coming Next

- Improved billing and payment handling
- Enhanced activity logs and tracking
- Better reporting and insights
- More flexible payment features

---

Thank you for being part of the early journey 🚀
